atksurface

Mapping what attackers see first.

atksurface is an independent security research effort focused on external attack surface management — discovering internet-facing assets, identifying exposed services and misconfigurations, and verifying findings without disrupting the systems involved.

How we work

Non-destructive by design

Observation and safe verification only. No exploitation, no denial of service, no data modification.

Authorized scope only

We assess only assets we own or are explicitly authorized to assess.

Evidence over claims

Every finding is backed by verifiable evidence. When something could not be checked, we say so.

Responsible disclosure

Issues found in third-party products are reported privately to the vendor first.
